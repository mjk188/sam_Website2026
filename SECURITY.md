# Security, misuse and takedown

## Reporting a security problem

If you find a security issue on the website (https://samfoundation.in), please report it privately through the
contact page of the website. Do not open a public issue or post details publicly.

## Reporting copying, misuse or impersonation

All content in this repository and on the website is the exclusive property of Sam Foundation (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).
If you find our content, name or logo used elsewhere without permission, or any website, account or person claiming
to represent Sam Foundation, please tell us through the contact page with links and screenshots.

Sam Foundation never asks for payments, donations or personal details except through the official channels listed
on its website.

## Takedowns

Sam Foundation enforces its rights, including by filing DMCA and equivalent takedown notices with hosting
providers, platforms and search engines against infringing copies.
